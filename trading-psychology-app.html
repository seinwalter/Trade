ABSOLUTE HARD RULES - NO EXCEPTIONS EVER

  1. No trading first 15 minutes of session opening
  2. No trading without completed pre-trade checklist
  3. No trading past 2:30 PM Central time
  4. No trading on Fridays
  5. No trading on Holidays or Half Days
  6. Maximum 2 trades per day (during recovery phase)
  7. Must wait 30-45 minutes minimum between trades
  8. Exit immediately if uncomfortable after entry
  9. No trading on days with emotional state 7/10 or higher
  10. No trading after any loss until next day (recovery phase)
  11. ALL three timeframes must align - no exceptions
  12. Minimum 2:1 reward/risk ratio on every trade

Trading Recovery Protocol - Phase 1 (CURRENTLY IN EFFECT)

Position Sizing Rules

  • Start with 0.25 of normal size (micro positions)
  • Must achieve 3 consecutive profitable trades before any size increase
  • Each size increase limited to 25% of previous size
  • Reset to micro size after ANY two consecutive losses

Daily Trading Limits

  • Maximum 2 trades per day
  • Minimum 45-minute gap between trades
  • No trading after any loss until next day
  • Must achieve profitable week before weekend trading allowed

Advancement Criteria - Phase 2 Requirements

  • 3 consecutive profitable weeks
  • Consistent rules adherence above 90%
  • Can then trade at 50% of normal size
  • Maximum 3 trades per day allowed

Circuit Breakers - Immediate Trading Stop

Daily Circuit Breakers:

  • Two consecutive losses
  • Three total trades for the day (or 2 during recovery protocol)
  • Daily loss limit of 2R exceeded
  • Emotional state above 7/10 at any point
  • Breaking any trading rule
  • Unable to focus on charts properly
