# Trading Psychology Application

## Quick Start Guide

### Opening the Application

1. Navigate to the Trade folder
2. Open `trading-psychology-app.html` in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
3. The application runs entirely in your browser - no internet connection required after opening

### First Time Setup

The app automatically initializes with:
- Recovery Protocol Phase 1 active
- Maximum 2 trades per day
- Micro position sizing (0.25x normal)
- All safety protocols enabled

---

## Daily Workflow

### Step 1: Morning Routine (Pre-Market Tab)

1. Click **Pre-Market** tab
2. Start the **15-minute Mental State Check timer**
3. Complete ALL fields:
   - Sleep hours
   - Emotional state (1-10)
   - Stress level (1-10)
   - Sobriety status
   - Personal obligations

**IMPORTANT:** If your emotional state is 7/10 or higher, you will be blocked from trading.

4. Complete the Previous Day Review
5. Start the **15-minute Technical Analysis timer**
6. Document your analysis:
   - Daily trend
   - 4-hour structure
   - Key levels
   - Trading plan
   - Risk limits

7. Click **Save Pre-Market Analysis**

### Step 2: Trade Entry (Trade Entry Tab)

1. Click **Trade Entry** tab
2. Complete **ALL checkboxes** in the Pre-Trade Checklist
   - Timeframe Alignment (5 checkboxes)
   - Technical Confirmation (5 checkboxes)
   - Risk Management & Logistics (7 checkboxes)

3. Fill in Trade Details:
   - Symbol, Entry Time, Direction
   - Trade Thesis (explain WHY)
   - Position size, Entry, Stop, Target
   - Risk and Reward amounts

4. The R:R ratio calculates automatically
5. Click **Submit Trade Entry**

**Emergency Exit Button:** If you feel ANY discomfort after entering, click this immediately.

### Step 3: End of Day (End of Day Tab)

1. Click **End of Day** tab
2. Review today's trades summary
3. Check if any circuit breakers triggered
4. Complete reflection:
   - Rules adherence percentage
   - Biggest win
   - Biggest challenge
   - Plan for tomorrow

5. Click **Save End of Day Review**
6. Optional: Click **Export Today's Log as PDF**

## Key Features Explained

### Circuit Breakers

The app automatically enforces circuit breakers:

**Daily Circuit Breakers:**
- Two consecutive losses → No more trading
- Max trades reached (2 in Phase 1) → No more trading
- Any loss in recovery phase → No more trading until tomorrow
- Emotional state 7/10+ → No trading allowed

When a circuit breaker triggers:
- Red warning banner appears
- Trade entry is blocked
- Must wait until next day

### Timers

Built-in countdown timers ensure you:
- Spend full 15 minutes on mental state check
- Spend full 15 minutes on technical analysis
- Don't rush your preparation

### Data Persistence

- All data saved to browser's local storage
- Data persists even after closing browser
- No cloud sync - all data stays on YOUR computer
- Export functionality for backups

### Emergency Exit Tool

- Click when feeling uncomfortable in a trade
- Prompts you to document WHY
- Counts toward your statistics
- Reinforces that this is a STRENGTH

## Troubleshooting

### "Pre-Market Check Required" Warning

**Solution:** Go to Pre-Market tab and complete all fields, then click Save.

### Circuit Breaker Won't Clear

**Solution:** Circuit breakers reset the next day. This is by design - if triggered, you're done for the day.

### Data Not Saving

**Solution:**
- Make sure you're clicking the Save buttons
- Check browser isn't in private/incognito mode
- Browser local storage must be enabled

### Can't Enter Trade (All Checkboxes Checked)

**Solution:**
- Verify R:R ratio is at least 2:1
- Ensure pre-market analysis is saved
- Check that circuit breaker isn't active
- Verify emotional state is under 7/10
